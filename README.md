# Elena Paspel

**Learning Experience Designer (LXD) & Independent Researcher** in adult education, digital and AI literacy, and digital health communication.

I design online learning for adults, grounded in adult education theory and practice: andragogy (Knowles), experiential learning (Kolb) and outcomes-based design (Bloom's taxonomy, constructive alignment). As a researcher, I study how peer mentorship, human and AI-supported, builds lasting digital competence.

## Featured project

**[Personal AI Mentor](https://github.com/elena-paspel/personal-ai-mentor)**: an open-source prototype (v0.7) of a Socratically scaffolded AI mentor that supports learners' own reflective inquiry into a real task or problem they choose. Built from my peer-reviewed conceptual architecture.
